No emoji, no checkboxes, no substitutes. Where a mark needs to stand out it earns it through weight and contrast.

Faithful to the method

Most digital bullet journals quietly swap Carroll's glyphs for checkboxes and coloured icons. Journlet keeps them. Where something needs to stand out it earns it through weight and contrast, never by changing what a mark means.

Yours before it is ours

The journal lives on your device and works with no connection at all. Sync is a convenience layered on top, not the thing holding your entries together.

Unreadable to us

Entries are encrypted on your device before they are sent anywhere, so the server holds nothing it can read. It does hold your email address and a little bookkeeping, and this page would be wrong to pretend otherwise. What is stored, exactly.

Journlet installs from the app itself, not from this page. Open app.journlet.com, then:

iPhone and iPadTap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
AndroidTap Install when Chrome offers it.
Mac and WindowsUse the install icon in your browser's address bar.

Something wrong, or something missing?

Email a person

hello@journlet.com. A person reads it. Bug reports, things the notation gets wrong, features you keep reaching for and cannot find: all welcome, and none of it needs to be polite about it.

From inside the app

If the app opens for you, Menu → Send feedback is quicker. It fills in the build and the sync state for you, shows you the whole report before it goes anywhere, and sends nothing from your journal.

About your data

For anything about your data rather than the app, including deletion, privacy@journlet.com is the right address, and the privacy page says what is held and for how long.
