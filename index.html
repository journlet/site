The notation

Six marks. Not one of them reinvented.

  • •TaskSomething to do
  • ○EventSomething that happens
  • —NoteSomething worth keeping
  • ×CompleteStruck through, not hidden
  • >MigratedMoved forward on purpose
  • <ScheduledSent to the future log
  • *PriorityThe one that matters
  • ―No emojiNo checkboxes, no substitutes

Faithful to the method

Most digital bullet journals quietly swap Carroll's glyphs for checkboxes and coloured icons. Journlet keeps them. Where something needs to stand out it earns it through weight and contrast, never by changing what a mark means.

Yours before it is ours

The journal lives on your device and works with no connection at all. Sync is a convenience layered on top, not the thing holding your entries together.

Unreadable to us

Entries are encrypted on your device before they are sent anywhere. The server holds nothing but ciphertext. That is a property of the architecture, not a promise in a policy.
